Why an independent advisor matters

A plot is one of the largest, least liquid decisions you will make — and unlike an apartment, a flaw in the title, conversion or approval can be impossible to undo. Property portals and developer sales teams are paid to close a deal. My job is the opposite: to protect your capital, even when the honest answer is "don't buy this one." That single difference is why serious buyers, NRIs and family offices work with an advisor.

How I help

  • Location & corridor strategy — which Bangalore micro-markets (North Bangalore, Devanahalli, the airport & STRR belt) fit your goal and horizon.
  • Due diligence — title chain, EC, Khata, DC conversion, DTCP/BMRDA/BDA approvals and RERA verification before you commit.
  • Pricing & appreciation read — an honest view of fair value and realistic upside, not brochure projections.
  • Deal structuring — documentation, timelines and follow-through you don't have to chase.
  • Remote & NRI buying — the full process for investing in Bangalore plots from anywhere, safely.

Who I work with

First-time buyers taking a serious step into land; investors building a plotted-real-estate position; NRIs and HNIs who need a trustworthy pair of eyes on the ground; and family offices structuring land aggregation.
