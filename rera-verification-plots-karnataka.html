RERA Verification for Plots in Karnataka

A RERA number printed on a brochure proves nothing until you've matched it against the regulator's own record. I've seen numbers that belonged to a different project, plans that didn't match the registration, and "registered" layouts that simply weren't. The Karnataka RERA portal makes this a five-minute check that any buyer can do — and skipping it is one of the cheapest mistakes to avoid. Here's exactly how to verify a Bangalore plotted project, step by step, and how to read what you find.

Why verify, not trust

RERA registration only protects you if the project is genuinely registered and the details match what you're being sold. A number on a hoarding can be wrong, outdated, borrowed from another project, or attached to a plan different from the one on site. Verification on the official portal closes that gap — it confirms the registration is real, current and consistent with the layout you're buying into. It costs nothing and takes minutes; not doing it is pure, avoidable risk.

The step-by-step process

The Karnataka RERA portal is public and searchable. Work through it in order:

  • Step 1 — Open the portal. Go to the official KRERA site at rera.karnataka.gov.in and switch the language to English if needed.
  • Step 2 — Go to Registered Projects. Open the "Registered Projects" section, which lists every project registered under Karnataka RERA.
  • Step 3 — Search. Enter at least one identifier — the RERA registration number (fastest and most exact), or the project name, application number, promoter name, district or taluk.
  • Step 4 — Open the project record. Review the listed details: project name, promoter, project type (it should read as a plotted/layout project), status, area, expected completion date and the registration number.
  • Step 5 — Download the certificate & documents. Download the registration certificate and any approved plans, and read the registered completion date — that's the date the law binds to, not the brochure's promise.

How to read the results

Finding the project is only half the job — the details have to match. Cross-check every key field against what the developer told you:

The portal also surfaces the promoter's wider footprint — their other registered projects and any complaints recorded against them. That track record is free intelligence; read it.

Red flags to catch

Verification exists to surface exactly these problems:

  • No match — the number returns nothing, or a different project entirely.
  • Plan mismatch — the registered layout differs from what's being sold on site.
  • Lapsed or expired registration that the developer is still marketing as current.
  • Promoter-only registration — the agent or promoter is registered but the specific project isn't.
  • Pattern of complaints against the promoter on the portal.
If any of these appear, pause the transaction. A RERA discrepancy is rarely a clerical accident on a serious project. The wider warning list is in my red flags guide.

Beyond RERA verification

RERA verification confirms the project is registered and consistent — it does not confirm a clean title. Pair it with the rest of the trail: the title chain and mother deed, a clean 30-year Encumbrance Certificate, the DC conversion order for ex-agricultural land, and the Khata. Frequently asked questions

How do I verify a plot's RERA registration in Karnataka?

Open the official KRERA portal (rera.karnataka.gov.in), go to Registered Projects, and search by RERA number or by project name, promoter, district and taluk. Confirm the name, promoter, type, status and approved plan match, then download the certificate and read the registered completion date.

What is the Karnataka RERA website?

The official portal is rera.karnataka.gov.in. It lists registered projects and agents, lets you verify a registration number, download certificates, and view a promoter's other projects and complaints. Use the English language toggle if needed.

What should I check on the RERA certificate?

That the number matches the brochure, the promoter and project name are correct, the status is active, and the registered completion date and approved layout match what you've been told — plus the promoter's record. Then pair it with independent title, EC, conversion and Khata checks.
