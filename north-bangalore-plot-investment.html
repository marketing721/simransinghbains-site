North Bangalore Plot Investment Guide (2026)

If one direction has carried Bangalore's land story for the last decade, it is north. The airport reshaped the map, and the infrastructure following it has made North Bangalore the city's most watched plot belt. Here is why — and how to buy here without getting burned.

SS
Simran Singh Bains — Investor, plot developer & investment consultant. 20+ years, 1,000+ acres closed.

The drivers

  • Kempegowda International Airport — the anchor that pulls jobs, hotels and business parks north.
  • STRR & the airport metro — regional road and rail connectivity still being built out.
  • Business & aerospace ecosystem — the IT investment region and industrial parks creating end-user demand.
  • Relative value — still more affordable per square foot than mature east/south pockets.

The key pockets

Devanahalli — the headline corridor (see the dedicated Devanahalli guide). Yelahanka — mature, dependable, well-connected. Doddaballapur Road and the airport-influence belt — earlier-stage, higher-patience plays.

Who it suits

Patient investors riding infrastructure-led appreciation, end-users planning future homes near the airport, and NRIs/HNIs building a North Bangalore position. Land here rewards holding power, not quick flips.

Before you buy

Most of this belt was agricultural, so the paperwork matters more than the brochure: BMRDA approval + release certificate, RERA registration, DC conversion, a clean 30-year title and EC, and A-Khata. Full list in the document checklist.
