About

A long career, briefly stated.

Over twenty years, I have closed more than a thousand acres of land, structured forty million square feet of real estate transactions, and delivered fifteen thousand homes and commercial units. The lesson, after all of it, is unglamorous: the best investments are the quietest ones — patient, well-underwritten, built on conviction rather than noise.

That conviction shapes every decision today. Across real estate, renewable energy, artificial intelligence, and growth-stage businesses — the sectors differ, the standard does not. Clear thinking. Honest counsel. Capital that respects its outcome.

I work with families taking their first serious step into real estate, institutions looking for a thoughtful partner, and founders building businesses meant to outlast their first round of applause.
