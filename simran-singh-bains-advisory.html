What I do

I give investors a straight answer to three questions that decide every plot purchase: where to buy, what to verify, and how and when to exit. That means reading the micro-market honestly, running the legal due diligence properly, and matching the corridor to your budget, horizon and risk appetite — not selling you whatever inventory happens to be available.

  • Corridor & micro-market guidance — which Bangalore/India corridors fit your goals, using the six-driver framework.
  • Due-diligence review — a second pair of eyes on title, EC, approvals, conversion and khata, against my legal checklist.
  • Buy-side strategy — entry timing, price sanity, and structuring for appreciation versus end-use.
  • Exit planning — holding period, liquidity and how to sell well.

Who I work with

Three kinds of investors, mostly. First-time plot buyers who want to avoid the expensive mistakes — the wrong corridor, the bad title, the overpriced entry. NRIs investing from abroad who need someone on the ground to verify what they cannot see and handle the India-specific paperwork (see NRI plot investment and the rules on NRIs buying plots). And experienced investors and families building a longer-term land position who want disciplined corridor selection and clean execution. If you are weighing a specific plot or corridor right now, that is exactly the conversation I'm useful for.

How the advisory works

It is deliberately simple and unsentimental:

  • 1. Brief — you tell me your budget, horizon, risk appetite and goal (appreciation, end-use, or both).
  • 2. Corridor shortlist — I score the relevant micro-markets and give you a ranked shortlist with the reasoning.
  • 3. Plot-level review — for the options you like, I review the documents and flag exactly what to verify or avoid.
  • 4. Decision — you buy with a clear view of the upside, the risks and the paperwork — or you walk away with conviction.

Why independent advice matters

Most "advice" in real estate comes from someone who earns a commission on the specific inventory they are recommending. That is not advice — it is sales. The value of an independent, experienced view is that the answer can be "don't buy this," or "this corridor is already priced in," or "the title on this one won't hold." Over a 5–10 year hold, the corridor you choose and the plots you avoid matter far more than the brokerage on any single deal. My incentive is your outcome and your next referral, not today's commission.

How I think about your money: land is a long game. The goal is not to win the fastest trade — it is to own clean, well-located land in a corridor with real drivers, bought at a sane entry, and to exit it well. Everything I advise serves that.

Frequently asked questions

What does a plot investment advisor actually do?

An independent plot investment advisor helps you decide where to buy, what to verify and how to exit — reading the micro-market, running legal due diligence on title and approvals, and matching the corridor to your budget and horizon, without being tied to selling any particular inventory.

Do you work with NRI investors?

Yes. A large part of my work is helping NRIs invest in Indian plots from abroad — verifying on the ground what they cannot see, handling the India-specific documentation and approvals, and selecting corridors suited to a long-distance, long-horizon hold.

Why use an independent advisor instead of a broker?

A broker typically earns a commission on the specific inventory they recommend, so their incentive is to sell it. An independent advisor can tell you not to buy, that a corridor is over-priced, or that a title won't hold — which over a long hold matters far more than any single brokerage.

How do I start working with you?

Get in touch with your budget, horizon, risk appetite and any specific plot or corridor you're considering. I'll come back with a ranked corridor shortlist and, for options you like, a document-level review of what to verify before you commit.
