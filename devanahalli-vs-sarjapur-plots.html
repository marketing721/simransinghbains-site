The case for Devanahalli

Devanahalli stacks an unusual number of drivers — the international airport, the Satellite Town Ring Road, the airport metro and an aerospace/business ecosystem. That has driven roughly +98% over five years. It suits patient investors who can ride infrastructure as it matures, and who want maximum appreciation rather than immediate use. Full detail in the Devanahalli guide.

The case for Sarjapur Road

Sarjapur sits in the heart of Bangalore's IT belt, with deep, durable employment demand and some of the strongest medium-term land movement in the city — on the order of +134% over three years. It suits investors who want appreciation plus genuine livability and a deep resale pool. See the Sarjapur Road guide.

Which should you choose

  • Pure appreciation, long horizon, can wait → Devanahalli.
  • Appreciation + livability + liquidity → Sarjapur Road.
  • Lower entry point per sq ft → Sarjapur stretches often start lower.
  • Airport-led mega-infrastructure bet → Devanahalli.

Whichever you pick, the deciding factor is the same: a clean title and the right approvals. A great corridor can't fix bad paperwork — run the checklist.

FAQ

Which appreciates faster, Devanahalli or Sarjapur?

Both have moved strongly; Sarjapur has shown sharper three-year gains, Devanahalli strong five-year gains. The right pick depends on entry price, horizon and whether you value end-use.

Which is cheaper?

Sarjapur stretches often start lower per sq ft, but rates vary widely by exact location and approvals.
